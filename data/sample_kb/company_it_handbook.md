# NovaRetail Internal IT Support Handbook

## VPN Access
Employees working outside the office must use the company VPN before accessing internal systems. Install the NovaSecure VPN client from the internal Software Portal. Sign in with your company email and password, complete MFA, and select the closest corporate gateway. If the connection fails, first confirm internet connectivity, restart the client, and try another corporate gateway. Do not disable endpoint security or firewall settings to make the VPN work. If the problem remains, open an IT ticket with a screenshot of the error.

## Password Reset Policy
Company passwords must be at least 14 characters. Employees should use the self-service identity portal for normal password resets. After five failed login attempts, the account may be temporarily locked. If self-service recovery fails, contact IT Service Desk. Employees must never share passwords or MFA codes with support personnel.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for email, VPN, cloud administration, and other protected company systems. The approved methods are the company authenticator app and registered hardware security keys. SMS should only be used when explicitly enabled as a recovery method.

## Wi-Fi and Network Connectivity
When working from a corporate office, devices must connect exclusively to the 'NovaRetail-Secure' wireless network using your standard active directory credentials. Guests and personal mobile devices must use the 'NovaRetail-Guest' network, which requires authorization via a browser splash portal page. Direct Ethernet connections at desks are restricted to provisioned desktop workstations; do not unplug infrastructure cables to connect personal laptops.

## Device Procurement and Hardware Lifecycle
All standardized computing hardware is assigned based on role tier allocations managed by the IT Procurement team. Laptops are subject to a mandatory hardware refresh lifecycle every three years. If hardware exhibits severe performance degradation or physical damage before the scheduled refresh cycle, submit an engineering asset review request signed off by your direct manager. Lost or stolen hardware assets must be reported to the Corporate Security hotline within two hours of discovery.

## Offboarding and Data Retention
Upon termination of employment or contractor contract completion, access to corporate infrastructure, email accounts, and identity directories is revoked immediately at the offboarding hour designated by Human Resources. All assigned corporate assets—including laptops, monitors, peripheral access keys, and mobile devices—must be surrendered to the IT asset department within 3 business days. User data stored on local machines is wiped immediately upon return, while data stored inside personal cloud drives is archived and transferred to the line manager for a standard retention duration of 90 days before final purge.
